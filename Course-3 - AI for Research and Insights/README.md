# 📖 About

**AI for Research and Insights** is the third course in the **Google AI Professional Certificate** program.

This course focuses on using **Google Gemini** to conduct comprehensive AI-assisted research, synthesize information from multiple sources, generate structured research reports, and develop customized AI experts. It also emphasizes evaluating AI-generated responses by verifying sources and grounding outputs in reliable documentation.

The course equips learners with practical techniques to improve research efficiency while ensuring the accuracy, transparency, and trustworthiness of AI-generated insights.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this course, I can:

- Conduct AI-assisted research using Gemini Deep Research.
- Generate comprehensive research reports from multiple sources.
- Extract meaningful insights and key themes from diverse information.
- Create and manage research hubs using Gemini Notebook.
- Summarize multimodal content effectively.
- Build customized AI experts for domain-specific tasks.
- Verify AI-generated responses using trusted source documentation.
- Produce reliable, evidence-based insights for decision-making.
