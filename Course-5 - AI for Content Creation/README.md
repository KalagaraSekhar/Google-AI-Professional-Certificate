# 📖 About

**AI for Content Creation** is the fifth course in the **Google AI Professional Certificate** program.

This course focuses on leveraging **Google Gemini** to generate high-quality visual content, design engaging presentations, and refine creative assets using AI-powered feedback. It introduces practical workflows for image generation, presentation enhancement, and creative review while emphasizing responsible AI practices and maintaining brand consistency.

The course provides hands-on experience in transforming ideas into professional visual content using Generative AI.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this course, I can:

- Generate high-quality images and videos from creative concepts using Gemini.
- Design visually engaging presentations with Gemini in Google Slides.
- Develop consistent AI-generated content aligned with brand guidelines.
- Critically evaluate AI-generated creative assets.
- Improve visual designs using structured AI feedback.
- Apply multimodal prompting techniques for content creation.
- Use Responsible AI principles in creative workflows.
