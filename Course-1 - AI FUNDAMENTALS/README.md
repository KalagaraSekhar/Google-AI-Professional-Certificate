# 📖 About

**AI Fundamentals** is the first course in the **Google AI Professional Certificate** program.

This course introduces the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI while focusing on practical workplace applications. It emphasizes effective collaboration with AI systems, prompt engineering fundamentals, responsible AI usage, and integrating AI into professional workflows.

The course provides a strong conceptual foundation for the remaining courses in the certification program.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this course, I can:

- Understand the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence.
- Explain how Generative AI works.
- Collaborate effectively with AI tools.
- Design structured prompts for better AI responses.
- Improve prompt quality using prompt engineering.
- Evaluate AI-generated responses critically.
- Identify bias and limitations in AI outputs.
- Apply Responsible AI principles.
- Integrate AI into everyday professional workflows.
